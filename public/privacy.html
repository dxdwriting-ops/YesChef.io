YesChef.io privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. Replace the contact address below with your own before publishing.

What the game stores

Your chef profile (name, outfit, level, coins, collection, quest progress and settings) is saved only in your own browser using local storage. It never leaves your device unless you play online, as described below. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Playing online

When you join a kitchen, the game server receives the chef name and outfit you chose, your position in the kitchen and your actions, so other players can see you. This is used only to run the game and is not stored after the room closes. The server, like any website, may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the service running and secure.

Advertising

On our own website (not on itch.io) the game may show ads from Google. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and opt out of personalised ads at adssettings.google.com. Rewarded ads are always optional.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Chef names are free text, so please don't use your real name.
